Privacy Policy

Maia by Aether Corp · Last updated [date]

The short version

  • Your photos are analyzed, kept encrypted until the end of your day (never more than 24 hours), then permanently deleted from our servers.
  • We keep the written examination (scores and a short note) so you can track progress. You can delete it at any time.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use your photos to train AI models.
  • You can delete your photos, examinations, chat history or your whole account from Account → Privacy.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, a securely hashed password, the date you confirmed you are 18 or older, and whether your email is verified.
  • Profile: the goals, preferences, sensitivities, budget, region and climate you choose to share.
  • Photos (optional, with your consent): skin, hair or posture photos (standing, in clothing) you choose to upload.
  • Examinations: the written scores and notes created from each photo.
  • Rituals and activity: your generated rituals, the steps you check off, and messages you send to Maia.
  • Subscription status: your plan, renewal date and which store you paid through. Card and payment details are handled by Stripe, Apple or Google; we never see or store them.

Your photos

A photo is only uploaded if you tick its consent box. It is encrypted (AES-256) as soon as it reaches our servers, used to create your examination and today's ritual, and permanently deleted at the end of your local day, and in every case within 24 hours. Photos are stored separately from the rest of our data and are never included in our backups.

If you turn on "Keep a copy on this device", a copy is saved only in the app's storage on your device. It is never uploaded again, it is excluded from iCloud and Google device backups, and you can delete it from the Progress screen.

Photos are sent to our AI provider, [Anthropic], only to produce your examination and today's ritual. The provider processes them under its commercial API terms, which do not allow training on your data. How we use your information

We use your information to create your rituals and examinations, answer your questions, run your subscription, keep the service secure (for example, limiting repeated login attempts), and send account emails such as email verification. Maia offers wellness guidance only; it does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run Maia: AI processing ([Anthropic]), payments (Stripe, Apple, Google, RevenueCat), email delivery ([Resend]) and hosting ([hosting provider]). Each processes data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

  • Photos: until the end of your local day, and never more than 24 hours.
  • Everything else: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Login sessions expire after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can delete photos, examinations and chat history, or your entire account, directly in the app under Account → Privacy. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, California, or the UAE and other GCC countries), you may have further rights to access, correct, export or restrict your data. Contact us at [privacy@yourdomain] to use them.

Security

Photos are encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, login tokens are stored hashed, and all traffic uses HTTPS.

Adults only

Maia is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
